About Glenda's Bakery

Baking joy into every loaf, cake, and pastry

Our Story

Glenda's Bakery was established in 2018 by Glenda Mthembu, a passionate baker who turned her love for baking into a thriving community business. What started as a small home-based operation has grown into a fully-fledged bakery with a storefront in the heart of Soweto.

Today, we employ five local community members and source ingredients from local suppliers whenever possible.

Our Mission

To bake joy into every loaf, cake, and pastry by using only the finest ingredients and delivering exceptional quality with a warm, personal touch.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted and beloved bakery in Gauteng, known for quality, community, and innovation in baking.
