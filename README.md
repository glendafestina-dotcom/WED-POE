## Project Overview
This project involves designing and developing a professional website for Glenda's Bakery, a small family-owned business based in Soweto.

## Website Goals
- Increase sales and orders by 25% within 6 months
- Showcase products with descriptions and pricing
- Build brand awareness and trust
- Simplify customer enquiries

## Key Features
- 5-page website: Home, About, Products, Enquiry, Contact
- Navigation menu linking all pages
- Product showcase with images and pricing
- Enquiry form for custom orders
